AN IMMERSIVE CELEBRATION

Where Ancient Heritage Meets Festival Energy

RAAS LEELA is an opulent Navratri evening curated for lovers of Garba, Dandiya, and cutting-edge sound. Step into a starlit palace atmosphere filled with rhythmic clacks, booming basslines, and celebratory joy in Hubli.

Royal Atmosphere

A dark, luxurious sanctuary with ambient illumination, velvet hues, and celebratory decor.

High-Energy Beats

Celebrity sensation DJ AURA takes command of the arena, fusing timeless Gujarati folk with festival drops.

Grand Raas Circle

Thousands of dancers moving in synchronized Dodhiya and Popat circles until midnight.

Traditional Maha Aarti

Auspicious ceremonial Aarti honoring Maa Durga to sanctify the arena before the night's celebration.

Grand Food Court

Savor authentic Gujarati delicacies, Navratri special treats, street food favorites, and refreshments all evening.
