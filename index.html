WHAT TO WEAR

Royal Dress Code & Styling Guide

🥻 👘 🥻

Embrace the royal Gujarati & Rajasthani festive spirit! Dress in your most radiant traditional attire to stand out and win the best-dressed awards.

🥻

For Women & Girls

  • Attire: Multi-color Chaniya Choli with mirror-work (Abhala), embroidered Ghagra, or royal Bandhani Lehengas.
  • Jewelry: Chunky oxidised silver jewelry, maang tikka, waist belts (kamarbandh), and colorful glass bangles.
  • Colors of the Night: Deep Maroon, Wine Crimson, Burnt Orange, Mustard Gold, and Peacock Green.
  • Footwear: Flat embellished juttis or mojaris for comfortable non-stop dancing!
👘

For Men & Boys

  • Attire: Traditional Gujarati Kedia with Kafni pyjama, royal Kurta-Pajama with Bandhani/Kutch jackets, or Pathani suits.
  • Accessories: Colorful Kathiawadi turbans/safas, brooches, pocket squares, and leather mojaris.
  • Colors of the Night: Royal Maroon, Midnight Blue, Saffron, Golden Ochre, and Off-White Silk.
  • Tip: Breathable cotton or silk blends ensure maximum comfort during high-energy steps!
✨

Rules of the Arena

  • Dandiya Sticks: Wooden or fiber dandiyas are permitted. Metal/pointed objects are strictly forbidden.
  • Western Formals/Casuals: Festive ethnic fusion is welcomed; however, traditional ethnic wear gets priority for awards!
  • Props & Accessories: Glow bands and small LED accessories are welcome!
  • Baggage: Please carry minimal belongings; a safe cloakroom is provided near the helpdesk.
