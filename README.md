## ✨ Key Interactive Features
1. **Live Countdown Timer:** Counting down to October 13, 2026, 6:00 PM IST.
2. **Synthesized Ambient Garba Groove:** Web Audio API sound engine playing an authentic 3-Taali rhythm with toggleable controls and animated sound bars (zero external MP3 required).
3. **Interactive Dandiya Beat Simulator:** Tap the left/right Dandiya sticks (or use keys `A` and `L`) to clack, build combo chains, and charge the Garba energy meter from Warmup to Godmode Taal!
4. **Pass Booking & Dynamic E-Pass Generator:**
   - Multi-tier pass selector with live total pricing calculations.
   - Generates an official **Royal Digital E-Pass with Scannable QR Code** rendered on HTML5 Canvas.
   - Built-in **Print / Save as PDF** support.
   - One-click **WhatsApp Confirmation** link pre-filled with the Attendee Name, Phone, and unique Pass ID.
5. **Official Poster Showcase:** Lightbox zoom modal featuring the official event creative (`poster.jpg`).
6. **DJ Aura Spotlight & Soundboard:** Featured playlist highlights and interactive Dhol drop preview buttons.
7. **Interactive Venue Schematic & GPS Navigation:** One-click Google Maps link with key landmarks and distances from Hubli Junction, Airport, and Chennamma Circle.
8. **FAQ Accordion & Styling Guide:** Clear dress code advice for men and women, venue entry guidelines, and answers to common queries.
9. **Mobile-Responsive Sticky Action Bar:** Bottom bar with quick pass pricing and WhatsApp booking for maximum mobile conversions.
